Part C · Short Answer 3 points each

  1. Absolute location is an exact coordinate position on Earth — a specific x- and y- value. Relative location describes where something is in relation to other things.
    Rubric: 1 pt correct definition of absolute; 1 pt correct definition of relative; 1 pt for a valid example of each. Accept any reasonable examples — the book uses coordinates typed into Google Maps (absolute) versus a driving instruction such as “turn left at the Valencia Blvd exit,” or giving directions to your house using landmarks (relative). (p. 14)
  2. Raster and vector.
    Rubric: 1 pt for naming raster; 1 pt for naming vector; 1 pt for a correct distinction. Raster models are grid images made of rows and columns of pixels, each georeferenced and holding a numeric value — most remote sensing imagery enters a GIS this way. Vector models are built from georeferenced points, which connect into lines and close into areas (polygons), each carrying an attached database. Full credit for capturing “pixels/grid” versus “points, lines, and polygons.” (p. 21)
