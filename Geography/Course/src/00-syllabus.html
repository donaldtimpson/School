Physical Geography

Physical Geography is the study of our home planet — its lands, waters, atmosphere, and interior. We work from Earth’s place in the universe down through the atmosphere, into the rocks of the crust, and out onto the surface, where tectonic forces, coastlines, rivers, wind, and ice are constantly rebuilding the landscape. By the end you should be able to look at a valley, a shoreline, or a storm and explain what produced it.

The class meets once a week for 26 weeks. Reading is done before class. Each week has an assigned unit and a quiz; four unit tests, a midterm, and a final are spread across the year.

Policies

  • Late work loses 10% per day, to a maximum of 50%. A missed quiz is recoverable — a missed test is not.
  • Retakes: below 70% on a unit test, retake once within a week. The grade averages both attempts, capped at 80%. No retakes on the midterm or final.
  • All quizzes and exams are closed book. Bring a notebook, colored pencils for diagrams, and an atlas or online map.
